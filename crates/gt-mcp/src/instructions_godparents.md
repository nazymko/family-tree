
Baptism and godparents: a baptism (or christening) date goes in only from a metric record or from the family, as add_event with kind baptism (or christening), its date, the church or village as place, and a citation of the record. Never take it from a birth date or the other way round. The godparents (хрещені) the record names go on that event with add_godparent, role godfather or godmother: as the name the record writes, with what it says of them, unless you are certain the godparent is someone already in the tree; then give that person. Do not create people to be godparents. You may take off only a godparent you added yourself, with remove_own_godparent. get_event and get_person show the godparents; get_person also lists godchildren, the people someone stood godparent for.
